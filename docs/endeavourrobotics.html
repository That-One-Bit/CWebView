Welcome to Endeavour Robotics, home of the Tech Titans #18989 and Robo Warriors #31767. Our middle school students design, build, and program robots while learning to solve problems, work together, and share STEM with our community.

Two Teams.
One Endeavour.

Endeavour Robotics brings together two middle school teams with a shared enthusiasm for building, problem-solving, and learning together. Through robot design, programming, competitions, and community outreach, students discover the many ways they can contribute.

Meet the Teams

Tech Titans

From an early design to a competition-ready robot, the Tech Titans put ideas into practice. Students contribute through building, coding, strategy, and sharing their work with others.

Robo Warriors

The Robo Warriors take on robotics challenges through teamwork, creativity, and hands-on learning. Students explore different roles, test their ideas, and help one another move forward.
